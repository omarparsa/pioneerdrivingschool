Requirements

DMV permit and license checklist.

Use these common California requirements to prepare for training and the behind-the-wheel test.

DMV Permit Requirements

  • You must be 15 1/2 years old.
  • Bring an original or certified copy of a U.S. birth certificate, passport, or green card.
  • Have your Social Security Number.
  • Complete the Driver Education Certificate requirement.
  • A parent or legal guardian must sign the acknowledgement statement before your DMV visit.
  • Complete the DMV application.
  • Pass your vision test and DMV permit test.

DMV Driver License Requirements

  • You must be 16 years old.
  • Hold your permit for at least 6 months.
  • Make an appointment for the DMV driving test.
  • Complete at least 50 hours of supervised driving, including 10 hours at night.
  • Complete 6 hours of behind-the-wheel training with a driving school.
  • Pass the DMV driving test to obtain your California driver license.
FAQ

Common questions before you book.

Quick answers for parents, teen drivers, adult learners, and students preparing for the DMV test.

Do I need a permit before behind-the-wheel lessons?

Teen students need a valid California learner permit before starting behind-the-wheel instruction. Adults can call us to confirm the right next step for their situation.

Do you offer pickup and drop-off?

Pickup and drop-off service is available in select service areas. Availability depends on instructor schedule, location, and lesson package.

Can nervous adult drivers take lessons?

Yes. Lessons are customized for new, nervous, returning, and senior drivers who want calm coaching and practical road confidence.

Do you help with DMV test preparation?

Yes. We offer focused practice for DMV test skills, common mistakes, parking, lane changes, observation habits, and test-day confidence.

How many hours do teen drivers need?

California teen drivers commonly need 6 hours of professional behind-the-wheel training before licensing. Extra practice may help students who want more confidence.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Book Online button to choose an available appointment, or call us if you want help selecting the right package.
